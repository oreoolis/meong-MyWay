## Video direction

Bright, executive, and human. Use MyWay’s white canvas and slate ink with cobalt-blue
as the primary focus color; reserve orange and green for route distinctions. Editorial
serif accents introduce the promise, while precise sans and mono labels make the agent
system and analysis feel operational. Use smooth long-tail motion (`power3` register),
revealing each information cue across the full scene instead of front-loading. Motion
traces paths, handoffs, and evidence; holds permit subtle status motion only, then settle.
Frame 4 is the deliberate breather with a long static lockup. Keep every critical element
inside the top 83% safe zone. Never use generic AI orbs, stock photography, unsupported
claims, slideshow front-load/freeze motion, or screensaver-style independent floating.
No narration, captions, music, or sound effects.

MyWay

A personalized career coach.

One resume. Six agentic AI agents. A connected career plan.

The six agents are Resume Parser, Questionnaire Agent, Career Planner,
Resume Improver, Industry Advisor, and Career Swapper.

MyWay turns a resume into grounded career paths, skill gaps, resume rewrites,
live role matches, and a contextual analysis chatbot that can explain the plan.

Core product language from the app:
- Understand your experience, with context.
- Map paths, not job listings.
- Act on advice from specialists.
- Your career, mapped around you.

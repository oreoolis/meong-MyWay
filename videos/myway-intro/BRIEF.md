## Intent

A concise, professional product opener for the start of a live pitch. Establish
MyWay as a personalized career coach, reveal the six-agent AI system behind it,
then show the quality of analysis and conversational guidance it produces.

## Assets

- `../../../frontend/meong-my-way/src/app/globals.css` — source of the MyWay palette, typography, and motion language.
- `../../../frontend/meong-my-way/src/components/` — source of the product's real analysis, results, and chat UI patterns.

## Customizations

- Four scenes: personalized coach, six agentic AI agents, analysis/chatbot outcomes, and a product-showcase close.
- Use a polished editorial-to-product-UI visual arc with MyWay's white, ink, blue, orange, and green system.
- Keep the piece narration-free so a live presenter can speak over it. HyperFrames reported no signed-in or installed local music provider, so this run is intentionally silent.
